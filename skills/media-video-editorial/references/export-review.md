# Review the actual export

Use this reference for production or review of a real encoded video. Scope checks to the requested deliverable and available viewing tools. A script-only commission needs no fictional export checklist.

## Content and audiovisual continuity

Open the exact exported version. Watch the sequence and listen to the entire take using an available playback/listening capability. Check pronunciation, missing or duplicated words, unnatural cuts, pace, intelligibility and audio-to-scene meaning. If actual listening or playback is unavailable, retain the audio/video and mark that check unperformed. Transcription, loudness measurements and file metadata can reveal defects, but cannot prove a performed listening pass.

At each transition, compare the spoken claim with the image and text on screen. Confirm that a source image is not being used to imply a different population, event or measured outcome. Check whether viewers can identify the necessary detail before the scene changes. Contact sheets help locate visual defects; they do not establish motion, audio or timing quality.

Review caption wording and actual cue boundaries against the recording, especially after cuts, pauses and script changes. Whole-paragraph transcription timings are provisional until checked at cue level. Verify that source labels and meaningful qualifications survive the export.

## Destination and delivery

Inspect each requested variant at its actual destination crop and a realistic small viewing size. Check overlaps between editorial text, subtitles, credits, logos and platform controls. Test captions on/off where relevant; a clean master and a burned-caption master are different deliverables. Label desktop responsive previews separately from a native mobile-app check. Do not claim inaccessible device testing.

Check file integrity, duration, dimensions, aspect ratio, sound, subtitle tracks and the exact file handed over. A matching filename does not establish identical content; retain its hash and source lineage. Confirm the receiving workflow can open the asset or supported delivery reference. Keep private production files separate from the public payload.

## Review result

For each material finding retain the actual version, time range, observed defect or successful check, correction and recheck. Separate content, audiovisual, destination and delivery readiness. State which requested outputs are ready and which depend on a missing capability; do not turn missing evidence into a pass. Re-review affected scenes and variants after a correction, preserving unrelated approved work.

Audience retention can later motivate a new hypothesis. It cannot identify a scene's causal effect without an appropriate comparison; processing or unavailable metrics remain unknown.
