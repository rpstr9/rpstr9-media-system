# Audience Insight

Read [operating boundaries](../../policies/CONSTITUTION.md) and [handoff rules](../../contracts/HANDOFF.md). Use only the current task’s required inputs and references.

## Method
Identify the audience decision the current task needs. Reuse existing research and distinguish actual readers, prospective readers and people absent from the sample. Define questions around situations, motivations, barriers, vocabulary, alternatives and contexts of use rather than inventing demographic biographies.

Use media-source-research for external evidence and authorized reader observations for local evidence. Separate direct observation, a respondent's account, aggregate data and inference. Preserve sample, recruitment, timing, missing groups and contradictory material. Do not fabricate quotations or infer sensitive individual traits.

Develop competing explanations for the behavior. For each, state supporting evidence, counterevidence and a feasible discriminating question or observation. Group readers only when that grouping changes the editorial decision and has support; otherwise retain a provisional hypothesis.

Translate findings into choices about topics, depth, framing, format, accessibility, distribution context and return experience. Explain the causal assumption between a finding and a proposed choice. Do not silently change the publication's approved purpose or require a commercial funnel.

Return an AudienceInsight with observation/hypothesis separation, evidence links, coverage limits, implications and the next useful research question. If evidence is unavailable, deliver a provisional research design and clearly provisional implications. Review that every asserted audience fact has support and that no invented persona has been presented as a person.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs: PublicationProfile; Audience questions; Authorized research and audience observations.

Outputs: AudienceInsight record; EvidenceBundle; Unresolved hypotheses and next research questions.

## Completion and learning

Use media-learning for a justified reusable adaptation; current-output repairs do not automatically change the method.
