# Experimental edition 0.1.8

This edition carries approved strategy into editorial selection, research, writing and production through explicit editorial foundation records. Discovery articles must deliver a supported change in understanding within the article; reference articles must help readers complete their task accurately. Examples, experiments and visuals are optional and must make a concrete reader contribution. The no-padding rule applies across workflows. Publication subjects, examples, visual styles and owner exclusions stay in private context. No model is pinned.

Growth centers on potential-reader research and actual conversation, with brief contextual responses, verified actions and a stopping rule across runs. Resource research supports that loop when the publication needs it. Creation and launch bundles include distribution, relationships and measurement. The owner's authority remains separate from planning or execution evidence.

Supplied: 20 reusable skills, 21 complete workflow reading bundles, 58 record schemas, editorial and launch records, a private SQLite reference timer, action reconciliation, scoped local learning and staged release adoption. Package/schema/source-integrity checks are exposed development fixtures; passing them does not establish editorial quality, reader benefit or live provider delivery.

Prior execution evidence: 35 runtime checks and eight browser-host protocol checks passed on the preceding runtime implementation. A finite synthetic timer exercise processed 70 occurrences across two private workspaces, including stop/restart and five simulated effects per workspace. The demonstration executor and bundled write adapter are synthetic. A live scheduled browser send and a permanent background service have not been exercised by this library release. Budget reservation, deadlines and finite retries are implemented; a trusted host must enforce actual provider charges. The subprocess boundary is not an OS sandbox, and pause cannot undo an already submitted remote action.

Scheduled operation can use a Codex host turn with computer/browser tools or a configured command runner. Interactive use requires the actual account, capability, current platform requirements and owner authority. Instructions alone are not credentials or a security boundary.

Existing strategy, perception-flow and identity methods remain external. Their approved outputs can be imported; invoking a method requires its complete source. A fresh full external strategy/identity run and two live publication launches were not evaluated. Nested strategy and identity payloads need method review beyond schema validation. Public fixtures are exposed author-reviewed evidence, not an independent benchmark.

Newly authored library files retain the repository's MIT license. External methods keep their own reuse terms. Resolve published content through releases/latest.json; private evidence, adaptations and publication assets are excluded.
